# 0.3.x

> **Beta.** Everything under 0.3.x is an unfinished preview: breaking changes can
> land at any time, saved servers or local data may need to be wiped between
> builds, and anything documented here can change before a stable release.

## 0.3.3 — 2026-10-09

Updates the bundled interface to `naigi-frontend` 0.4.3. Still **Naigi Beta**.

### Fixed

- Your own camera left your tile blank: the tile replaced its avatar with your video, but your camera was
  published to a separate preview box. Local and remote video now follow one rule — a camera goes into
  its owner's tile, a share goes into the share box.
- The share box was not cleared when you left a room, and each rejoin added another picture to it. The
  box is emptied on leaving, and every render reconciles it against the live subscriptions.
- Camera failures now name their cause instead of a single "could not start" line.

### Added

- A stream quality picker beside the camera and share controls: Low (360p, 15 fps), Standard (720p,
  24 fps) and High (1080p, 30 fps), each labelled with its data cost. The choice is remembered on this
  device and applies the next time you start a source.
- Screen share now asks for audio, so a shared tab can be heard where the platform allows it.

## 0.3.2 — 2026-10-09

Updates the bundled interface to `naigi-frontend` 0.4.2. Still **Naigi Beta**.

### Fixed

- **The green speaking border disappeared around participant tiles.** The local preview was positioned
  absolutely with no positioned ancestor, so on a phone it escaped the voice panel and covered the tiles
  underneath it, taking the speaking border with them. The preview now sits in the panel's own flow.
- A participant's camera no longer disappears when the room re-renders.

### Changed

- A participant's camera replaces their profile box on their tile, with their name and connection state
  underneath. Their avatar shows only when no camera is running.
- A screen share gets its own box above the participant list, full width and letterboxed, separate from
  the voice and camera tiles. Someone who is sharing still gets a mark on their tile.

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-09

Updates the bundled interface to `naigi-frontend` 0.4.1. Still **Naigi Beta**.

### Fixed

- **The camera could not be turned on.** The page asked the WebView for the camera without taking the
  native permission first, which Android refuses without explanation. Camera permission is now
  requested in context when you turn the camera on, and a permanent refusal points at system settings
  instead of prompting again for something that cannot appear.
- The native permission bridge resolved its platform plugin only once, at page load. On a slow start
  the plugin had not registered yet, so the bridge stayed unavailable for the whole session and every
  permission request did nothing. It now looks the plugin up on each request.

### Added

- A local preview of your camera or shared screen while it is running, muted and never published, so a
  misframed camera or the wrong screen is visible to you before it reaches the room.
- A persistent banner while your camera or screen is live, naming what is being sent and offering to
  stop it.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-09

Updates the bundled interface to `naigi-frontend` 0.4.0. The app still installs as **Naigi Beta** and is still a preview.

### Added

- **Camera in voice rooms.** A camera control beside the microphone, plus a switch-camera control. Camera permission is requested only when you turn the camera on — never at launch — and the camera is released as soon as you turn it off or leave the room. Both controls are 44px touch targets in the dock.
- **Receiving video.** A participant's camera and screen share appear above the participant list while they are publishing, sized so the dock and the list stay reachable on a phone.
- **Screen sharing control that tells the truth.** The Android System WebView has no screen-capture picker, so on this app the control is present but disabled and says screen sharing is not available here, rather than failing after you tap it.

### Security

- Camera and screen video are published on the same encrypted connection as the microphone. Nothing is sent before encryption is confirmed, and there is no unencrypted fallback.

### Known gaps

- Android screen sharing needs native MediaProjection support and is not implemented. Camera works.
- Video is shown in the voice room panel only, and screen sharing does not carry system audio.
